Executive Summary

Built on Precision Formulation.

Founded over four and a half decades ago in Karachi, Mumtaz Ink Co. has established itself as a trusted name in the ink manufacturing industry. Built on continuous innovation and rigorous quality standards, we deliver high-performance ink solutions tailored for offset press requirements.

Under CEO Arham bin Mumtaz's leadership, the company meets the rigorous demands of high-speed commercial presses, packaging converters, and educational publication houses across Pakistan.

Core Product Offerings

Engineered for high-speed performance and chromatic accuracy.

Flagship #1 Offset Black

Signature high-density black ink formulated for deep jetness and crisp line sharpness. Features fast setting times, minimal rub-off, and superior runnability on high-speed presses.

High-Performance CMYK Process Series (RRR Grade)

Precision-balanced four-color process inks for vibrant, color-accurate reproduction. Includes Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Bronze Red shades providing superior transparency and high gloss.

Korean Addition Series (MPI Korean Line)

Enhanced formulations using imported Korean additives and pigments for specialized performance. Superior chemical resistance, enhanced lightfastness, and exceptional gloss for demanding substrates.

PREMIUM GRADE

Rotary News Press Inks

Heavy-duty, high-volume inks engineered for coldset and heatset rotary web offset presses. Low misting at high speeds and immediate absorption on newsprint.
